# music_disc_generator
A Minecraft utility to add new Music discs to your game

Minecraft Music Disc Generator is a powerful and user-friendly utility designed to create custom music disc resource and data packs effortlessly. Ranging from version 1.21.1 up to 26.4, this tool automates the entire generation process so you can get your custom tracks in-game in no time!

> ℹ️ **Version Note:** The GUI currently displays **26.3 (latest)** in the version selection dropdown. Selecting **26.3** generates packs fully compatible with **26.4** without issues, as the underlying `jukebox_song` registry format remains identical across both releases.

---

### ✨ Features
* **Wide Version Support:** Generates packs compatible with Minecraft versions 1.21.1 all the way up to 26.4.
* **Drag & Drop Support:** Simply drag and drop your audio files directly into the utility for quick processing.
* **Full Customization:**
  * Custom Namespace selection
  * Custom Music Disc textures
  * Configurable song titles, lore, and metadata
* **In-Game Preview:** Built-in preview feature letting you see exactly how your custom music disc will look in-game before exporting.
* **Advanced Options:**
  * **Custom Crafting Recipes:** Automatically generate recipes for your music discs so players can craft them in survival.
  * **Creeper Loot Integration:** Option to add your custom music discs to the creeper loot table (when blown up by a skeleton/stray).

---

### 🚀 Getting Started
1. Download the latest release of the utility.
2. Launch the application and drop your audio files into the drop zone.
3. Configure your disc settings (namespace, custom texture, item name, etc.).
4. Toggle your preferred options (recipes, creeper drop loot, etc.).
5. Select your target Minecraft version (use **26.3** for 26.4 target builds).
6. Preview your disc and hit **Generate** to export your ready-to-use resource and data pack!
