# music_disc_generator
A Minecraft utility to add new Music discs to your game
Minecraft Music Disc Generator
​A powerful and user-friendly Minecraft utility designed to create custom music disc resource and data packs effortlessly. Ranging from version 1.21.1 up to the newest 26.3, this tool automates the entire generation process so you can get your custom tracks ingame in no time!
​✨ Features
​Wide Version Support: Generates packs compatible with Minecraft versions 1.21.1 all the way up to 26.3.
​Drag & Drop Support: Simply drag and drop your audio files directly into the utility for quick processing.
​Full Customization:
​Custom Namespace selection
​Custom Music Disc textures
​Configurable song titles, lore, and metadata
​In-Game Preview: Built-in preview feature letting you see exactly how your custom music disc will look in-game before exporting.
​Advanced Options:
​Custom Crafting Recipes: Automatically generate recipes for your music discs so players can craft them in survival.
​Creeper Loot Integration: Option to add your custom music discs to the creeper loot table (when blown up by a skeleton/stray).
​🚀 Getting Started
​Download the latest release of the utility.
​Launch the application and drop your audio files into the drop zone.
​Configure your disc settings (namespace, custom texture, item name, etc.).
​Toggle your preferred options (recipes, creeper drop loot, etc.).
​Preview your disc and hit Generate to export your ready-to-use resource and data pack!
